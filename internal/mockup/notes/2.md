# Motions

Motions resolve to a target and a kind. Operators consume that. `dw`, `d2w`,
`3j` and `dd` are one path rather than four special cases, so a new motion
works under every operator the moment the resolver knows it.

1. resolve the motion
2. hand the operator a range and a kind
3. let the operator decide linewise or charwise
