# Buffer

Wrote the buffer by hand. `bubbles/textarea` has no per-character styling hook
and no modal editing, and syntax highlighting and vim motions both need to own
the buffer and the render path, so there was nothing to borrow.

`Buffer` holds `[][]rune` and `Pos.Col` counts runes. Not bytes, not cells.
Display width enters once, at render time, through `runewidth`.

That distinction has bitten me twice. A wide glyph is one rune, one column in
the buffer, and two cells on screen.
